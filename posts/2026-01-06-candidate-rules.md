# Candidate Rules

Essential guidelines and commitments that all candidates must adhere to when participating in the Multi-Religion Election System.

Important Guidelines

## Candidate Rules & Commitments

All candidates from different religions must adhere to the following fundamental rules that form the foundation of the Multi-Religion Election System.

### 1\. Church-State & Religion-Politics Separation

Addressing the incomplete separation in EU governance

#### The Current Problem

Christian EU citizens have their own Christian parties (e.g., Christian Democrats) that represent them religiously in the EU Parliament, while other EU citizens belonging to any other religion lack their own religious parties and thus have no representation in the EU Parliament.

#### Our Commitment

All candidates must acknowledge this imbalance and commit to working toward equal religious representation across all faith traditions in democratic institutions.

#### Candidate Requirements

- Acknowledge the incomplete separation between state and church

- Support equal political representation for all religious communities

- Advocate for religious neutrality in governance while respecting all faiths

- Work to ensure no single religion has preferential political representation

### 2\. Democracy in All Religions

Transitioning to the Multi-Religion Election System

#### Core Principle

It is democracy that has given all religious communities freedom of religion. Therefore, democracy must be introduced in all religions, without exception.

#### Implementation Strategy

- Countries with existing church elections (Nordic countries, Germany): Transition from church elections to Multi-Religion Election System

- Countries without such elections (USA, UK, China, India, Iran, Israel): Introduction of the Multi-Religion Election System from scratch

#### The Faith Representatives Chamber (FRC)

The FRC is free from political parties and politicians, replacing bodies like the General Synod. It is the highest body of all faiths, elected through Multi-Faith Elections.

#### Multi-Religion Support

A new financial model that replaces foundation-based and party support systems, operating independently of tax revenue to ensure true religious autonomy.

### 3\. Unique Conflict Resolution Model

Resolving the 77-year Israel-Palestine conflict

#### Our Mission

Apply the unique conflict resolution model so that the 77-year-old conflict between Israel and Palestine can be resolved without delay and prevent it from being passed on to the next generation.

#### How Democracy Resolves Conflict

- Replace religious extremism with democratic religious governance

- Give all faith communities equal voice and representation

- Remove political exploitation of religious differences

- Create interfaith dialogue through democratic institutions

#### Candidate Commitment

All candidates must commit to peaceful resolution, reject violence as a means of religious expression, and actively work toward reconciliation between conflicting religious communities.

### Key Democratic Institutions

New structures replacing outdated single-faith governance

#### Faith Representatives Chamber (FRC)

The highest democratic body for all faiths, elected through Multi-Faith Elections. Free from political party influence.

Replaces:

General Synod / Church Assemblies

#### Multi-Religion Elections

The practical application of the Multi-Religion Election System. Democratic elections open to all faith traditions.

Replaces:

Single-faith church elections

#### Multi-Religion Support

Independent financial model for all religious communities, free from tax revenue dependencies and political party control.

Replaces:

State religious foundations & party support
